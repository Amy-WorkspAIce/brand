# Logos

The mark is the Orbit: a sliced synthwave sun circled by a tilted cyan ring with one node, Amy. All SVGs have their text outlined and their gaps truly transparent, so they look the same in a browser, on GitHub and in design tools. Colors are fixed in each file (not `currentColor`).

- `amy-workspaice-lockup.svg`: the default logo, with the mark and wordmark side by side. For `night-000`, `night-100` and other dark grounds. Wordmark in `ink` #F5F0FF, hyphen `magenta` #FF2E97, ring and AI `cyan` #22E4FF.
- `amy-workspaice-lockup-light.svg`: the same lockup for light grounds. Wordmark in light `ink` #170C2B, hyphen #C2006A, ring and AI #006A80.
- `amy-workspaice-lockup-mono-white.svg`: single ink, white #FFFFFF. For dark grounds where color is off, or on a `magenta` fill.
- `amy-workspaice-lockup-stacked.svg`: mark above wordmark, for square spaces such as social avatars and splash screens. Dark grounds.
- `amy-workspaice-lockup.png`: the default lockup as a 448px-tall PNG with transparency, for tools that can't take SVG.
- `amy-workspaice-wordmark.svg` and `amy-workspaice-wordmark-light.svg`: the name alone, for places where the mark already appears nearby. Use at 120px wide or more.
- `amy-workspaice-mark.svg`: the Orbit alone, in a 64×64 box. Use it for avatars, Amy's presence in the UI and favicons above 24px. Dark grounds.
- `amy-workspaice-mark-light.svg`: the mark with a darker ring (#006A80) for light grounds.
- `amy-workspaice-mark-mono-white.svg` (#FFFFFF) and `amy-workspaice-mark-mono-ink.svg` (#170C2B): single-ink marks for stamps, embossing and one-color print.
- `amy-workspaice-app-icon.svg`, plus `-512.png`, `-180.png` (Apple touch icon) and `-32.png` (favicon): the Orbit over a magenta grid floor on a clipped `night-100` tile.
